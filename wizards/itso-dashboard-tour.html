Step 1 of 6

What the ITSO member dashboard is

The ITSO (Information Technology Systems Ontario) member dashboard is a launchpad giving you single-sign-on access to all the technology tools and services included with your membership.

  • The intro line confirms the key mechanic: Note “Select your product or service below to login using REALTOR.ca SSO” — click a tile and it logs you in automatically via your REALTOR.ca credentials.
  • Because of SSO, you don’t manage separate passwords for each tool.
  • A search box lets you quickly filter to a specific tool, and a top navigation bar (About, Access, Compliance, Digests) sits above the tiles.
Step 2 of 6

The top navigation bar

Four links across the top cover background, eligibility, rules, and announcements.

  • About — background on ITSO and the services it provides.
  • Access — eligibility and how to get into the various systems.
  • Compliance — rules, policies, and regulatory guidance; useful for confirming what you’re allowed to do with MLS data or advertising.
  • Digests — an archive of news bulletins and member communications; check here to stay current on system changes and announcements.
Step 3 of 6

Core products (the main grid)

This is the primary section — the tools you’ll use day to day.

  • Matrix — the MLS platform itself: searching listings, adding and editing your own listings, building CMAs, running market statistics, and setting up client auto-emails. The workhorse for almost any listing- or market-related task.
  • OneHome (Agent Portal) — the client-facing companion to Matrix where buyers receive and interact with the listings you send (favouriting, commenting, browsing). Use it to see how clients are engaging.
  • BrokerBay — a showing-management and scheduling platform for booking and coordinating showings, managing appointment requests on your listings, and handling feedback.
  • ShowingTime — another showing-coordination tool with a similar scheduling and appointment-management function, depending on which your brokerage uses.
  • SentriLock — the electronic lockbox system for managing lockboxes on your listings and gaining access to properties for showings, tied to your secure credentials.
  • CloudCMA — a dedicated comparative-market-analysis and report-generation tool for polished, client-ready valuation and listing-presentation reports.
  • SingleKey — a tenant/applicant screening service, most relevant for rental and lease transactions needing background or credit checks.
  • Bridge — a data/listing platform often used for data access and syndication. Prospects is a mobile CRM/contact-management app for managing clients on the go. Note ProspectsMobile is marked “Coming Soon,” so it’s not yet active.
Step 4 of 6

CREA & REALTOR.ca section

These tiles connect you to national-level systems.

  • CREA Members Area — the Canadian Real Estate Association’s member portal for national resources and account matters.
  • CREA WEBForms — the standard digital contract and forms platform for drafting and completing transaction paperwork.
  • REALTOR.ca Members Area — the back end of the public-facing REALTOR.ca site, where you manage how your listings appear to consumers.
Step 5 of 6

Resources section

These are reference and regulatory links rather than working tools.

  • OREA (Ontario Real Estate Association) — your provincial association for education and standard forms.
  • CREA — links to the national association’s main site.
  • RECO (Real Estate Council of Ontario) — the provincial regulator; go here for licensing, registration, and compliance matters governing your practice.
Step 6 of 6

When you’d use what — a quick mental map

Think of the dashboard in workflow terms, matching each tool to the stage of your deal.

  • Research & prep: use Matrix / CloudCMA for researching the market and preparing listings and valuations.
  • Buyer clients: use OneHome for working with and tracking buyer clients.
  • Showing logistics: use BrokerBay / ShowingTime / SentriLock for getting people into properties.
  • Paperwork: use WEBForms once a deal is moving.
  • Rentals: use SingleKey for the tenant-screening side.
  • Association & regulatory: use the CREA / OREA / RECO / REALTOR.ca links for association, regulatory, and consumer-facing matters.
