Setting Up Showings

Step 1 of 7

Complete Your Profile

A quick orientation: this is BrokerBay, the showing-management and lockbox platform, opened from your ITSO dashboard via REALTOR.ca single sign-on — so there’s no separate login. The left menu (Brokerage, Dashboard, Lockboxes, My Activities, Showings, My Favorites, Analytics, Contacts) is your main navigation; most setup lives under the top-right avatar menu and the Lockboxes section.

  • Click your avatar in the top-right corner and choose Account Settings to open your profile.
  • On the Profile tab, set your profile photo using the image upload area, then confirm your First and Last Name and optionally add a Pronoun.
  • Fill in the Contact section: Mobile #, Office Extension, Other Number, Direct line, and Email. Click Save when done.
  • Note This information is what other agents see and what’s used for showing notifications, so it’s worth getting accurate before anything else.
Step 2 of 7

Set Your Front Desk Instructions

Still in Account Settings, these settings tell your brokerage’s front desk how to handle calls and messages on your behalf.

  • Open the Front Desk Instructions tab.
  • Under Forward Office Messages, choose whether to release your mobile number, turn on call forwarding, forward calls, and set vacation dates for when you’ll be away.
  • Under Other, store a Copier/Printer Code and add any Front Desk Notes.
  • Tip Configure these to match exactly how you want to be reached during showings.
Step 3 of 7

Configure Your Default Listing Settings

Open the Listing Settings tab. As the page notes, these apply by default to all your new listings and can be overridden per listing later.

  • Feedback Collection — toggles to automatically collect showing feedback, get notified when new feedback arrives, and optionally auto-send that feedback to your sellers.
  • Automatic Merge Suggestion — email alerts about merging duplicate listings.
  • Expiry Notification — an alert when a listing is about to expire (e.g., within 7 days).
  • Tip Turning on feedback collection and seller-feedback forwarding is a common setup that saves manual work.
Step 4 of 7

Set Up Notifications

Control how and when BrokerBay alerts you about activity on your listings.

  • From the avatar menu, choose Notification Settings.
  • Configure alerts for incoming showing requests, feedback, and tasks.
  • Tip Set these so showing requests reach you promptly through your preferred channels.
Step 5 of 7

Add and Sync Your Lockboxes

This is the core lockbox step. Note that “Supra” appears as the branding here, but the lockbox brands this account integrates with are SentriLock and Master Lock — you’ll choose your specific brand during setup.

  • Click Lockboxes in the left menu, then + New Lockbox.
  • In the Lockbox Details form, open the Brand dropdown and select your brand (SentriLock or Master Lock).
  • When you choose SentriLock, BrokerBay looks up boxes registered to your account and shows them under Available Boxes (serial numbers). Click Sync Lockboxes to pull them in.
  • Note Boxes must already be assigned to you in your SentriLock account to appear here. If it shows “No Lockboxes found for user,” none are registered to your credentials yet — have them assigned in SentriLock first.
  • Once synced, each box appears in the Lockboxes table where you can assign it to a listing.
Step 6 of 7

Set Up Templates and Key Co-ops (Optional)

These optional refinements live in the avatar menu and can wait until the basics above are in place.

  • Template Manager — create reusable message and instruction templates to speed up showing communications.
  • Key Co-ops — manage key-sharing arrangements between offices.
  • Tip Skip these for now if you’re just getting set up; come back once profile, notifications, and lockboxes are done.
Step 7 of 7

Verify on the Dashboard

Return to Dashboard to confirm everything is working.

  • Check that your listings appear (for example, a 1095 Mineral Springs Road listing currently set with a Combination Lock Box).
  • Review the Showings (Buyer/Listing) and Tasks panels.
  • From here you can also use Create Listing and Merge/Combine Listing.
  • Once a lockbox is synced and assigned to a listing, showing requests and access flow through this dashboard.
  • Note On credentials & security: setup won’t involve entering lockbox PINs, access codes, or account passwords — those are sensitive and should be entered by you directly. Anything under the User Billing tab (payment details) is also yours to handle.
