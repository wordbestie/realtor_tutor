Step 1 of 5

Before You Start

This walkthrough covers a Master Lock box — opening it, setting a new combination, mounting it, and troubleshooting. The basic rhythm is the same across models: slide down the protective shutter, align the dials (or enter the code), press the release to open, insert your keys, and close firmly.

  • Identify your model: dial (rotating number wheels) or push-button (a keypad of buttons).
  • The factory default combination is often 0-0-0-0 — try that first if you haven’t set your own yet.
  • Tip Always scramble the dials (or clear the buttons) after every use so the code isn’t left exposed.
Step 2 of 5

Opening the Lock Box

How you open it depends on whether you have a dial or push-button model.

  • Dial models: Slide the weather/shutter cover down to expose the number dials.
  • Align your combination exactly with the center line, then press the release button (usually a large black button or lever) down and pull the door forward.
  • Push-button models: Press the CLEAR button first, then enter your designated code, and press down or slide the open lever to release the door.
  • Note If the door won’t budge, double-check that each dial is locked perfectly into the center track.
Step 3 of 5

Setting a New Combination

Change the code from the default to something only you and your office know. The box must be open first.

  • Dial models: With the box open, find the small reset lever on the back or side of the door. Push it to the left and down.
  • Turn the outside dials to your new desired code, then push the reset lever back to its original position (up and right). Close the door and scramble the dials to lock.
  • Push-button models: On the inside of the door, slide the pins/tabs to the SET position corresponding to your new numbers (codes can range from 1–12 digits depending on the model).
  • Close the door, test the new code, then slide the pins back to lock it in.
  • Tip Refer to your specific model’s instructions — pin layouts vary between Master Lock versions.
Step 4 of 5

Hanging or Attaching the Lock Box

Mount the box wherever the keys need to live — a doorknob, railing, or wall.

  • Shackle models: With the box open, find the shackle release lever inside (it usually pushes to the side) to free the metal loop.
  • Place the shackle over a doorknob or fence, snap it closed, then lock the compartment door.
  • Wall-mount models: Use the included mounting screws and anchors to attach the backplate to a wall, fence, or secure flat surface.
  • Note Make sure the box hangs where it won’t swing into glass or paint, and where showing agents can reach it comfortably.
Step 5 of 5

Troubleshooting a Stuck Lock Box

A few common fixes when the box won’t open.

  • Sticky button: The release can stick in cold weather, or when bulky items inside push against the door. Push firmly on the front door while pressing the release button down.
  • Dial alignment: Make sure each number is seated perfectly in the center track before trying the door again — a dial that’s slightly off will block the release.
  • Tip If it still won’t open, gently scramble and re-enter the code slowly, confirming each digit lands on the center line.
