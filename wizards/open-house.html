Post an Open House to a Listing

Add an open house to one of your listings in Matrix yourself — from finding the listing through to the “Input Succeeded” confirmation.

Open the listing for editing
Open the Open Houses form
Fill in the open house details
Step 1 of 4

Find the listing

Go to the Add/Edit tab in the top navigation. This opens the Modify Property search page.

  • In the Location section, type the street number into the St # field and the street name into the Street / Island field — for example, 1095 and Mineral Springs.
  • Click the Search button at the bottom of the section to find your listing.
Step 2 of 4

Open the listing for editing

When your listing appears in the results, you’ll see three edit links next to it: Data, Photo, and Sup.

  • Click Data to open the listing edit options.
  • This brings you to the Modify Listing page.
Step 3 of 4

Open the Open Houses form

On the Modify Listing page, scroll to the Select Form section and click Open Houses.

  • This opens the Manage Open Houses form, which shows any existing open houses plus a blank entry at the bottom for adding a new one.
  • The blank entry’s Status will say Active — that’s the one you fill in.
Step 4 of 4

Fill in the open house details

In the blank Active entry, fill in the details, then submit.

  • Enter the Date (e.g. 06/28/2026 for the upcoming Sunday) and choose the Open House Type (Public).
  • Set the Start Time and End Time: type each value (like “11:00” and “01:00”) and select the correct AM/PM radio button beside it. This is the easiest thing to get wrong — double-check that start is AM and end is PM for a late-morning-to-afternoon event.
  • Optionally add notes in Refreshments or Remarks. Don’t use commas in Remarks — REALTOR.ca doesn’t support them.
  • Click Submit Listing at the bottom. You’ll see an “Input Succeeded” confirmation, which means the open house was saved and will appear on the system shortly.
  • Tip There’s a Validate button next to Submit if you want to check for errors first, and each open house entry has its own Delete button if you ever need to remove one.
