Step 1 of 6

What the Branding Center is

The Branding Center (a CoreLogic/Cotality tool) is where you build a “Brand Kit” — your logo and colour scheme — that then applies automatically across the client-facing real estate apps connected to your account.

  • As the page puts it, you “manage your logo and colours shown across your real estate apps.”
  • The whole point is consistency: set your branding once here and it propagates everywhere your clients see you, rather than configuring each product separately.
  • The screen has two halves — a control panel on the left where you build the kit, and a live Sample View on the right that previews how your branding will look in each product before you commit.
Step 2 of 6

The building blocks of a Brand Kit

A kit has three components: a logo, a colour palette, and a review-and-save step.

  • Logo — uploaded via the “Drag image here / Upload from device” box. Recommended size is 525×150 pixels with a 10MB maximum.
  • Colour palette, split into three roles: the Primary colour is applied to banners and headers, the Secondary colour is for elements such as buttons, and the Accent colour is for smaller details.
  • Review & save — check the kit in the preview and save it.
  • Tip Prefer not to build from scratch? Available Brand Kits offers ready-made options, including an “Unbranded” default and pre-made kits you can search for.
Step 3 of 6

Where your branding shows up

The Sample View has three tabs, and each represents a real place clients encounter your brand.

  • OneHome — the client buyer portal. Your branding appears as the coloured header on the agent card and the agent profile block (name, brokerage, licence number, and photo), displayed alongside the listings clients browse.
  • Matrix Email — the listing/auto-emails clients receive. Your branding shows as the coloured header bar across the top, your photo and “From” name, and the styling of the property cards inside.
  • GoAgent — the mobile app view. Your branding appears on the home-screen header, the greeting, the profile photo, and the navigation/map-search elements.
  • Note In short, the kit drives your appearance in the buyer portal, the emails you send, and the mobile app.
Step 4 of 6

Step-by-step: customizing your branding

To build your own kit, start from the My Brand Kit area and click Create your own, which opens the “Create Your Own Brand Kit” panel.

  • Add your logo — click Upload from device (or drag an image into the box). Aim for roughly 525×150 pixels for the best fit and keep it under 10MB.
  • Set your colours — click Primary and choose the colour for banners and headers, then do the same for Secondary (buttons) and Accent (small details). As you make each choice, watch the Sample View update in real time.
  • Review across all three surfaces — click through the OneHome, Matrix Email, and GoAgent tabs to confirm the logo and colours look right in each, since the same kit renders a bit differently in each context.
  • Save & Activate — per the on-screen note, saving makes the kit “active across all your products,” so this one action pushes your branding to the portal, emails, and app together.
  • Tip While building, Reset clears your changes and Cancel closes without saving.
Step 5 of 6

Switching or turning off branding

You can revert or swap kits at any time from the Available Brand Kits section.

  • Select Unbranded (a clean default with no personal branding) or swap to a different pre-made kit.
  • Then hit Save & Activate to apply it.
  • Note Because activation is global, switching kits changes your look everywhere at once.
Step 6 of 6

A couple of practical notes

Since this kit becomes your public-facing identity to clients, a little care up front goes a long way.

  • Use a high-resolution logo at the recommended dimensions (525×150).
  • Choose colours with enough contrast that text stays legible on the coloured banners.
  • Tip “Save & Activate” applies immediately across all connected products — so preview all three tabs before saving, not after.
