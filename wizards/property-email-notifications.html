Setting Up Property Email Notifications

Create an automatic property email in Matrix — build a saved search and set it to email new matching listings to a contact on a schedule.

Step 1 of 9

Start a new search

From the top navigation, click Search, then choose Residential from the property category list.

  • This opens the Residential search criteria page.
Step 2 of 9

Enter the criteria

Fill in the price and room counts in the search form.

  • In the Price field (right column), type 1000-1500. The (000s) checkbox is checked, so this means $1,000,000–$1,500,000.
  • In Bedrooms type 3, and in Total Bathrooms type 2.
  • The Active and Conditional status boxes are checked by default — usually what you want.
Step 3 of 9

Set the location

In the City field (middle column, under Location), type Dundas.

  • A dropdown list appears — click Dundas in that list to select it.
  • The match counter at the bottom updates to confirm the filter is working.
Step 4 of 9

View results

Click the Results button at the bottom to see the matching listings.

  • A criteria summary appears at the bottom so you can double-check everything: Property Type, Status, City, Price, Beds, and Baths.
Step 5 of 9

Open the auto-email form

On the results page, click the Save tab, then choose New Auto Email.

  • This opens the “Save a New Auto Email” page.
Step 6 of 9

Create the recipient contact

Next to the Contact dropdown, click Create a New Contact.

  • In the popup, fill in First Name (Dave), Last Name (Zak), and Email Address (contact@davezak.com), then click Save.
  • The contact is automatically selected, and the “To:” line shows their email.
Step 7 of 9

Add a subject

Fill in the Subject field (required, highlighted yellow) with something descriptive.

  • For example: New Dundas Listings: 3 Bed / 2 Bath, $1M–$1.5M.
  • The message body and your signature are pre-filled automatically.
Step 8 of 9

Set the schedule

Scroll down to the Schedule section.

  • Daily is selected by default with all AM days checked, meaning the recipient gets a morning digest.
  • You can switch to ASAP (instant), adjust which days/times, or choose Monthly instead.
Step 9 of 9

Activate

Click Save at the very bottom. You’ll see a green confirmation banner (“Created auto email…”), which means it’s live.

  • Tip The bottom Save button sits close to the form content — make sure your click lands right on it, or the page just scrolls.
  • Note The recipient must have consented to receive these emails (Canada’s anti-spam law, CASL — Matrix reminds you of this). You can edit or pause any auto-email later under My Matrix → Auto Emails.
