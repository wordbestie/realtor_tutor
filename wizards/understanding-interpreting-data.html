Step 1 of 9

Getting to the Statistics module

Matrix keeps its market analytics under the MORE menu. Start here to open the statistics builder.

  • From the top navigation, hover over MORE, then STATS, and choose Matrix Stats. This opens the statistics builder.
  • The same menu also offers CREA HPI, MarketStats, and the Statistics Hub — covered in later steps.
  • The page is laid out with system presets on the left, your criteria filters in the centre and right, and output controls (Search, Chart, Data) at the top right.
Step 2 of 9

Defining your dataset (criteria)

Before running any report, tell Matrix which slice of the market to analyse — using the same filters as a property search.

  • In the centre and right panels, choose one or more Property Types (Residential, Commercial, etc.) and Sub-Types, and set Ownership Type if needed.
  • Define Price, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Total Sqft, and Acres Range as appropriate.
  • For location, use the Map Search, the radius “Within ___ km of” option, or the location fields (city, region, neighbourhood, postal code).
  • Tip Pick a segment specific enough to be meaningful but broad enough for a healthy sample size. A single street over one month gives noisy numbers; a neighbourhood and property style over a year is trustworthy.
Step 3 of 9

Running a preset report

The fastest path is the System Presets list on the left — run any of them against the segment you just defined.

  • With your criteria set, click any preset in the System Presets list to generate that report against your defined segment.
  • Available presets include: new listings by board, average price over the last twelve months, original price vs. sold price, days on market (DOM) vs. sold price, historic count of active listings, historic sales by year, historic sales price trend, historic sales by price range, monthly stats, and sale-price-to-list-price ratios.
  • After selecting one, click Generate (bottom left) to produce the result.
Step 4 of 9

Building a custom report

If no preset fits, the Customize tab lets you assemble exactly the view you want.

  • Click the Customize tab at the top.
  • Choose what to measure, how to group the results (for example by month, by area, or by price range), and how it should be charted.
  • This is useful for a specific combination — say, median sold price grouped by month for one neighbourhood.
  • Tip Click Save to store a custom report layout for reuse later.
Step 5 of 9

Viewing results as Chart or Data

Once a report is generated, switch between a visual and a tabular view depending on your purpose.

  • Use the toggle at the top right to switch between Chart and Data views.
  • The Chart view is best for presentations and spotting trends at a glance.
  • The Data view gives the underlying numbers in table form — better for precise figures or copying into your own analysis.
  • The Search toggle returns you to the criteria screen to adjust filters and re-run.
Step 6 of 9

Sharing or saving a report

Package a finished report for a client or a listing presentation using the toolbar tools.

  • The toolbar near the presets has print and email icons (and a Generate button).
  • Use these to email a pricing-trend chart to a client or print a market summary for a listing presentation.
  • Note The underlying detailed MLS data, including sold prices, is members-only and governed by MLS redistribution rules — reports are intended for your analysis and client advising, not wholesale republishing.
Step 7 of 9

Choosing the right tool for the question

Different tools answer different questions, so match the tool to your goal.

  • For raw transaction activity and segment-level detail (DOM, list-to-sold ratios, sales by price range), use the Matrix Stats module described above.
  • For a clean read on whether prices are genuinely rising or falling over time, use CREA HPI (MORE → STATS → CREA HPI) — its benchmark-based index smooths out the distortion that average and median prices suffer when the mix of what sold changes month to month.
  • For broader curated market reporting, use MarketStats or the Statistics Hub from the same menu.
Step 8 of 9

Interpreting the key metrics

A few reports are especially actionable — read them together for a rounded picture of pricing power and market velocity.

  • List-price vs. sold-price (sale-to-list ratio): tells you whether a segment favours buyers or sellers. Ratios near or above 100% signal a hot seller’s market; lower ratios suggest room to negotiate.
  • Original-price vs. sold-price: reveals how much sellers typically concede from their initial asking price — valuable for pricing a listing or framing an offer.
  • Days on Market: indicates how quickly that segment is moving.
  • Tip Reading these three together gives a well-rounded picture of pricing power and market velocity for any slice you define.
Step 9 of 9

A quick practical workflow

In practice, the efficient routine ties all of the above together into a few quick moves.

  • Open Matrix Stats and set your location and property criteria.
  • Click a relevant preset, then hit Generate.
  • Glance at the Chart view for the trend, then switch to Data view if you need exact figures.
  • Finally, print or email it if it’s for a client.
