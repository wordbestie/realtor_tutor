Every OREA Form Explained

OREA & TRESA forms for listing, selling, buying & leasing — plus FINTRAC. A quick reference, not legal advice.

★ Non-negotiable at the outset of every listing

Before anything — the TRESA gate

RECO
★RECO Information Guide
Mandatory consumer disclosure (a RECO document, not an OREA form) explaining representation, self-representation, and rights. Deliver and document acknowledgement before any representation agreement is signed — every new agreement, every time.
810 / 812
Working with a REALTOR® (1-page / 2-page)
Optional companion explainers for clients and self-represented parties.

Listing a property

200-series · one of 200 / 271 is your listing agreement
200
Listing Agreement — Seller Representation Agreement (Brokerage Representation)
Brokerage representation: the entire brokerage represents the seller, so same-brokerage deals on both sides = multiple representation. Every new listing or relisting needs a fresh agreement (new dates = new contract).
271
Listing Agreement — Seller Designated Representation Agreement
TRESA's designated model (new 2024): only the named rep(s) represent the seller; the brokerage provides services, not representation. Avoids multiple representation on same-brokerage deals. Your listing agreement at Real.
230
Listing Agreement — Seller Limited Services Representation Agreement
Restricted-scope listing (e.g., mere posting). With customer agreements discontinued under TRESA, this is the lighter-touch alternative to full representation.
209
Seller Direction to Share Substance of Offers
The open-bidding instrument: the seller's written direction authorizing disclosure of the substance of competing offers to other buyers. Pairs with 244 — together they document the offer-process election.
244
Seller's Direction re: Property / Offers
The seller's written instructions on offer handling, including delayed offer presentation. Required whenever offers are held. (224 is its companion information acknowledgement.)
220 / 221 / 222
Seller Property Information Statement (+ Condo / Water-Waste-Shoreline schedules)
Seller's voluntary condition disclosure; 222 matters for rural and waterfront properties. Optional — document the seller's decision either way. (225 = companion info sheet for sellers.)
299
Seller Disclosure of Latent Defect(s)
2024 form recording known latent defects the seller consents to disclose. Caution: widely criticized by real estate lawyers as overreaching — the law requires disclosing only known, latent, MATERIAL defects. Get legal advice before using.
240
Amendment to Listing Agreement
Price improvement, expiry extension, or change of designated representative on a live listing — without re-papering.
241
Suspension of Listing Agreement
Pauses marketing; the contract stays alive until expiry. For sellers who need a break without cancelling.
242
Cancellation of Listing Agreement
Ends the listing early by mutual consent. Watch: the holdover clause survives cancellation.
270
Open House Guest Registration
Visitor sign-in record for open houses — doubles as your lead capture.
MLS
★Cornerstone MLS Property Information Form (board document)
Your board's data-entry source for the broker load — the MLS listing must match it. Required for every listing; ITSO timing rule: in the system by 11:59 p.m. the business day after commencement.

Representing a buyer

300-series
300
Buyer Representation Agreement (Brokerage Representation)
The buyer-side contract under brokerage representation: term, geographic area, remuneration. Signed before showing or offering for a buyer client (RECO Guide first, as always).
371
Buyer Designated Representation Agreement
The designated-representation counterpart to Form 300 — only the named rep(s) represent the buyer. Pairs with 271 on the listing side; same multiple-representation logic.
301
Cancellation of Buyer Representation Agreement
Ends a BRA early by mutual consent — holdover survives here too.
310
Buyer Customer Service Agreement
Buyer as customer, not client — limited services without representation.
320
Confirmation of Co-operation and Representation
Confirms who represents whom and the remuneration between brokerages on a transaction. Accompanies every offer.

The deal itself

100-series · offers & conditions
100
Agreement of Purchase and Sale
THE offer — residential resale freehold. Price, deposit, irrevocability, completion date, conditions via schedules.
101
Agreement of Purchase and Sale — Condominium Resale
Condo version of the APS, with condominium-specific provisions including the status certificate condition.
105
Schedule — Agreement of Purchase and Sale
Blank schedule for additional terms and conditions attached to the APS — where your clauses live.
120
Amendment to Agreement of Purchase and Sale
Changes an accepted deal — price, closing date, or terms. Both parties must sign.
122
Mutual Release
Releases both parties when a deal collapses and directs the return of the deposit.
123
Waiver
A party waives a condition outright, keeping the deal alive without declaring it fulfilled.
124
Notice of Fulfillment of Condition(s)
Declares a condition satisfied. Read the condition's wording — it dictates whether a waiver (123) or fulfillment notice (124) is correct.

Leasing

400-series
400
Agreement to Lease — Residential
The lease offer; on acceptance, pairs with the Ontario Standard Lease.
410
Rental Application
Applicant's employment, income, references, and consent — your tenant screening record.
272 / 372
Landlord / Tenant Designated Representation Agreements
The designated-representation counterparts on the lease side — landlord (272) and tenant (372) versions of the 271 / 371 model.

FINTRAC

600-series · federal anti-money-laundering — the brokerage is the reporting entity
630
★Individual Identification Information Record
"The FINTRAC form": verified government ID, one per individual buyer or seller — every listing, at the outset. Prior valid verification at the same brokerage can be relied on if no doubts — confirm policy with your broker of record.
631
Corporation / Entity Identification Information Record
The 630 equivalent for corporate or entity clients, including beneficial ownership details — takes 630's place (and its star) when the seller is an entity.
632
Identification Mandatary / Agent Agreement
When someone else verifies identity on the brokerage's behalf — e.g., an out-of-town client identified by another agent.
634
Politically Exposed Person / Head of International Organization Checklist
Screens whether a client is a PEP or HIO, which triggers enhanced measures.
635
Receipt of Funds Record
Records deposit details on the buy side — required when a deposit lands in the brokerage trust account, even if you never touched the money.
639
Risk Assessment
Rates the money-laundering risk of the client / transaction for the brokerage's compliance program.
