Quick reference · the full rule set

The logo (the "real" wordmark)

  • It is a symbol only — because of the reversed "L," no real-estate commission treats it as a legible word.
  • Never coupled with text; never used as the word "Real" in the brokerage name.
  • Always inside a solid or outlined box on solid colour. Knockout / transparent version is allowed only over a photo.
  • Clear space all around equal to the wordmark's own height.

Your name

  • Solo agent = your name + a role designation. Never "[Your Name] Real Estate" (teams only, and they must add "Group" / "Team").
  • A role must appear under the name on signage.

Brokerage name

  • "Real Broker Ontario Ltd., Brokerage" spelled out in text on every piece, separate from the logo.
  • On signage it must be prominent — enlarged and running the full width.

Prospecting disclaimer

  • Cold/unsolicited pieces carry: "Not intended to solicit properties already listed for sale or buyers/sellers currently under contract."

Colour

  • Cobalt #050E3D and Chalk white #FFFFFF are the official Real colours.
  • Gold is a common personal accent, not a Real brand colour — fine as your own agent branding, just know it isn't theirs if they ever ask.

Fonts

  • Real official: Telegraf + Inter, or Poppins + Arial.
  • Your personal brand fonts are your choice — just keep Real's official pairings in mind on any strictly co-branded piece.
